How to play

Keep the fire alive until dawn. Walk over logs to pick them up (you can carry a few), then walk to the fire to feed it.

Move by tapping or dragging, or with the arrow keys or WASD. Stay inside the dashed ring of light: wolves attack you outside it and keep away from the fire.

P or Esc pauses. M or the speaker button changes the volume. After each night, choose an upgrade by tapping a card or pressing 1 or 2.

Top-left: the ring is the fire's fuel and the brown slots show the wood you carry. Top-right: the hearts are your health. The bar along the top fills as dawn gets closer.
